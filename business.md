# KnightSaver Business Experience

## Main bottom button

**+ List surplus**

When tapped, it opens three options:

- **Speak listing** — AI converts a voice note into a draft.
- **Quick form** — Staff enters listing details manually.
- **Use template** — Staff repeats a previous listing.

## Everything else on the same page

1. **Location and status**
   - Current business location.
   - Pickup service open/closed.
   - Closing time.
2. **Right now**
   - Portions available.
   - Portions reserved.
   - Students arriving.
   - Portions collected.
3. **Live listings**
   - Food name.
   - Remaining quantity.
   - Price.
   - Pickup deadline.
   - Available, fully reserved, or closed status.
4. **Pickup queue**
   - Student name.
   - Reserved item.
   - Payment status.
   - Contextual **Verify pickup** button.
5. **Waitlist**
   - Number of students waiting.
   - Active offers.
   - Released portions after cancellations.
6. **Today’s verified impact**
   - Portions rescued.
   - Food weight.
   - Revenue recovered.
   - Student savings.
   - Pickup completion rate.
7. **Recent activity**
   - Listing published.
   - Reservation created.
   - Payment authorized.
   - Student cancellation.
   - Payment released.
   - Pickup verified.
   - Payment captured.
